# Mistakes found in the AVVVS product catalogue

*Checked on 8 November 2026, against the live Shopify store (avvvsglobal.myshopify.com, 165 products).*

This document lists every problem found in the product content in Shopify, and what the new Ayur Store does about each one. The store now shows rewritten, grammatically correct content from `src/content/medicines.ts`, so customers no longer see the mistakes below. **The originals in Shopify are unchanged.** Fix them in Shopify too, because the old theme, Google Shopping and order emails still use them.

**For AVVVS’s pharmacist:**
- Please review sections 1, 2 and 5, and the per-medicine table at the end.
- Nothing medical was invented. Every use shown on the store comes from AVVVS’s own Shopify text.

## 5. Claims that are not suitable for a pharmacy listing

Several descriptions name serious diseases or promise cures. Examples:
- **COVID-19:** Relieve-O
- **Tumours:** Danthyarishtam, Lohasavam, Khadirarishtam
- **Neurological and psychiatric conditions:** autism, schizophrenia, Alzheimer’s and dementia (Saraswatarishtam); Parkinson’s (Aswagandharishtam, Kapikachu)
- **Infectious diseases:** leprosy and syphilis (Tiktaka Ghritham, Khadirarishtam); tuberculosis (six medicines)
- **Liver disease:** hepatitis and cirrhosis (Hepro)
- **Cure claims:** “curing gout” (Jatamayadi)

**Why this matters.** In India, the Drugs and Magic Remedies (Objectionable Advertisements) Act, 1954, and the rules on advertising Ayurvedic medicines, restrict how medicines may be promoted for many listed conditions. The listed conditions include diabetes, heart disease, blood pressure, obesity, infertility, sexual performance, epilepsy, paralysis and tumours. **Please have these listings checked by your regulatory adviser.** This document is not legal advice.

**What the store does now:**
- Every use is written as traditional Ayurvedic use (“Traditionally used for…”, “as supportive care alongside your doctor’s treatment”), never as a cure.
- The serious claims listed in the table below were **removed** from the store. The pharmacist can restore any of them.
- Sensitive but common uses were **kept and softened**, and flagged for review. These cover diabetes, heart, blood pressure, male vitality and menstrual health, and include Diastat, Katakakhadiradi, Parthadyarishtam, Desire, Ashwagandha and Kapikachu.
- Every product page carries a “Use with care” note, and links to a doctor’s consultation.

## 6. Store settings found while testing

1. **Checkout is blocked for guests.** The store’s checkout redirects to the classic customer login (`avvvsglobal.com/account/login`). In **Settings → Customer accounts**, make sign-in optional.
2. **No product tags.** None of the 165 products has a tag, so the store sorts medicines into concerns from its own starting map (`src/content/concernMap.ts`). After pharmacist approval, run `npm run tag-concerns -- --apply` to copy it into Shopify as `concern-…` tags. From then on, the tags in Shopify decide where each medicine appears.
3. **Medicine details are not structured.** Dosage, ingredients and anupana live only inside free text. The `pharmacy.*` metafields described in `SHOPIFY_SETUP.md` would let the store show them properly.
4. **Thirty products are out of stock.** “Notify me” requests are collected for them once `DATABASE_URL` or SMTP is set.
